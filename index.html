Jefferson Academy Secondary

Jefferson Academy (JA) is a school of excellence that works to prepare students for college, develop their character through rigorous expectations, and support them to discover and surpass their potential. As the oldest charter school in Jeffco, we have had years to grow and excel in preparing students for life beyond high school.

We are a close-knit community of young scholars who thrive in a challenging academic environment. By choosing JA, you will be a part of a community that feels like family, develops students with a strong work ethic and good character, provides a curriculum both rich in content and rigor, and has a staff devoted to providing the most innovative educational experiences to our students.
