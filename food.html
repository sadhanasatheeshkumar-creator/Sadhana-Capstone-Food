JA Food

Good nutrition helps kids thrive at school

Growing students need nourishing food to fuel their bodies and minds. Balanced meals and snacks can support steady energy, concentration, and healthy development throughout the school day.

  • Energy to learn A variety of nourishing foods helps students stay fueled for lessons, play, and everything in between.
  • Focus and wellbeing Regular meals and water help students feel ready to take part in the school day.
  • Healthy growth Different foods provide nutrients children need as they grow and develop.
